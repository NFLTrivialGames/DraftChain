NFL Draft Chain

How to Play ▾
  • Start by selecting your mode: Rookie Mode (2 matches required) or All-Pro Mode (3 matches required).
  • The game begins with a random college; your first player must come from that college.
  • Each subsequent player must match at least the required number of parameters (Team, Draft Year, Position, College, Round) with the previous player.
  • No repeated players.
  • You have 60 seconds per pick.
  • Click "Copy Chain" to save your current chain for bragging!
  • The goal is to create the longest chain possible.
